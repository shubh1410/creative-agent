## 300x250
Medium rectangle, close to square. Default frames: s1 → s2 → s3 → s4 → s5 (CTA) → end, about 2500 ms each.
Copy in the top half, product band in the bottom half, like the master. Corner logo: stacked 31x40.
End frame: stacked logo, 12.5% of width.

## 160x600
Wide skyscraper, the closest to the master's portrait shape. Default frames: all six.
Copy at the top, products in the lower band. Narrow: one or two words per line is fine; keep the headline to 2–3 lines.
End frame: stacked logo, 20% of width.

## 970x250
Billboard. Default frames: all six. Copy on the left 40%, products on the right 60% (reference landscape layout and the
brand's 60/40 grid). Keep text clear of the top-right logo box.

## 728x90
Leaderboard. Default frames: s1 → s2 → s5 (CTA) → end. One line of copy on the left, small product image in the
middle-right, CTA pill to the left of the logo box. No subheads. End frame: horizontal logo, at most 54px tall.

## 320x50
Mobile banner. Default frames: s1 → s5 (CTA, with its legal text) → end. One short line of copy plus the CTA pill;
no product images. Legal text at 8px on the CTA frame. Corner logo: horizontal, 18px tall.
End frame: horizontal logo, at most 30px tall.
